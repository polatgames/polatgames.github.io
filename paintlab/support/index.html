Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Hint/Undo/Route charges, streaks, records, themes and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app, or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back too.

Why do I see ads?

Paint Lab is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: watch one for an extra Hint, Undo or Route, to double the coins at the end of a level or a round, or to double your daily reward. A short ad may also appear from time to time after you tap “Next level” (never in your first five levels and never when you tap “Retry”) or when you leave the end-of-round screen of Speed Mode or Endless Mode — never while you are painting. A small banner sits in its own strip at the bottom of the game screen and the map, away from the maze and the buttons. Without an internet connection the game still works; there are just no ads, and video rewards are not available.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens iPhone Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Paint Lab. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Paint Lab follows your iPhone's language automatically. To pick another one, tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Language”, and choose from 19 languages (or “Phone language” to follow your iPhone again).

What does “Perfect” mean, and how are moves counted?

A move is one straight swipe that paints new boxes. When the brush changes direction, a new move starts. Sliding back over boxes you have already painted is free, so getting out of a dead end costs nothing. Every level has a Perfect number: the fewest moves anyone can paint it with. Finish with exactly that many moves and you get the golden “Perfect!” and three stars. Tip: keep going straight to the wall before you turn — turning early often costs an extra move later.

How do I get more stars?

Finishing a level always gives at least one star. Finish within a few moves of Perfect for two stars, and with exactly the Perfect number for three. Replay any level from the map: if you finish it in fewer moves, your stars go up. Using Route gives at most two stars for that try.

How do I unlock the next world?

Finish the Final (level 20) of the world before it and collect enough stars in total. If a world is still locked, its gate on the map shows how many more stars you need. The easiest way to earn them is to replay earlier levels and paint them Perfect.

Why does the brush slide when I tap a painted box?

That's the quick return! Tap any box you have already painted and the brush glides there along your paint, for free. It saves long walks back in big mazes. To keep painting, just swipe again from there.

How do free start and foggy mazes work?

Free start (World 3, Free Start): there is no brush at first. Tap the box where you want to begin — a smart start is the key to Perfect. Foggy mazes (World 4, Foggy Mazes): the whole maze is shown for two seconds, then fog covers it. Only the boxes around your brush and the boxes you have painted stay clear, so remember the way!

What do Hint, Undo and Route do?

  • Hint lights up the next stretch of the best route from where you are right now.
  • Undo takes back your last move: its boxes fade and the move counter goes down by one.
  • Route starts the level over and shows the whole Perfect route for about two seconds (that try gives at most two stars).

You start with 2 Hints, 2 Undos and 1 Route. When a helper runs out, get another one with coins, or watch a short video ad for a free one (look for the green play badge).

What are Speed Mode and Endless Mode?

Speed Mode: paint as many small mazes as you can in 60 seconds; a new one appears the moment you finish. Your best round is your record. Endless Mode opens after you finish World 2 (or all 100 levels): mazes keep coming and slowly grow. Each maze has a move limit (Perfect plus a few extra moves); the run ends when you go over it. Your score is the number of mazes painted in a row.

How do I unlock brush & paint themes?

Classic is yours from the start. Neon, Watercolor, Candy, Chalk and Space unlock when you finish the Final of Worlds 1 to 5, and Gold unlocks when you collect enough stars in total (the Collection shows how many). Open “Collection” on the home screen and tap a theme to use it — it changes the paint, the brush and the maze floor.

What are color-blind mode and left-handed mode?

Both are in Settings. Color-blind mode adds clear stripes to painted boxes, so you can tell painted from unpainted without relying on color. Left-handed mode swaps the order of the helper buttons and moves the move counter to the other side of the screen.

How do I connect to Game Center, and why might my score not show?

Game Center is optional; the game works the same without it. Sign in once in iPhone Settings › Game Center. After you have finished five levels, Paint Lab connects quietly on the home screen and a small Game Center button appears in the bottom-left corner of the home screen. Tap your Speed Mode record or your Endless Mode best on the home screen to open its leaderboard. If your score does not show yet:

  • you may not be signed in to Game Center, or Game Center may be restricted in Screen Time;
  • you may be offline — scores and achievements are kept on your device and sent the next time you are online and open the home screen;
  • only your best score counts, and leaderboards can take a few minutes to update.
