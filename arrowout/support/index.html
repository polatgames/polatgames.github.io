Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Hint/Undo/Blast charges, streaks, arrow themes, badges and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app, or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back too.

Why do I see ads?

Arrow Out is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: you can watch one for an extra Hint, Undo or Blast, for one more heart when you run out (once per level), to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily reward. A short ad may also appear between levels from time to time, but only after you tap “Next level” — never while you are playing, never after you run out of hearts or tap “Try again”, and not during your first five levels. A small banner sits in its own strip at the bottom of the level screen and the map, away from the grid and the buttons. Without an internet connection the game still works; there are just no ads, and video rewards are not available.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens iPhone Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Arrow Out. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear button on the home screen, tap “Language” and choose one of 19 languages. Choose “Phone language” to go back to your phone's language.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

Every level starts with 3 hearts. Tapping an arrow whose way out is blocked makes it bounce back, and one heart goes out — the thing in its way flashes so you can see why. When all 3 hearts are gone you can watch a video for one more heart (once per level), spend 60 coins for one more heart, try the level again, or go back to the map. If you continue, the grid stays exactly as you left it. Tip: Undo right after a bump gives the heart back.

How do I get more stars?

You get 3 stars when you clear a level without losing a heart, 2 stars if you lose one heart, and 1 star otherwise. Using Blast limits a level to 2 stars. You can replay any finished level from the map — your best result is kept, and new stars also give you coins.

How do I unlock a new world?

Each world opens when you finish the Final (level 40) of the previous world and have collected enough stars in total. Tap the locked world on the map to see how many more stars you need. If you are short, replay earlier levels to raise their stars.

How do long arrows, walls, spinners and locked arrows work?

  • Long arrows are 2–4 cells long and can bend. When you tap one, the whole arrow follows its head out of the grid, so its head's way out must be clear.
  • Walls are grey blocks that never move. No arrow can pass through them — find an order where every arrow has a way around.
  • Spinners are round tiles with a curved arrow. An arrow that slides over a spinner turns 90° (the way the spinner's arrow shows) and keeps going in its new direction.
  • Locked arrows carry a small padlock. The first tap unlocks them, the second tap slides them.

What do Hint, Undo and Blast do?

  • Hint lights up an arrow that can safely escape right now and shows its way out. If you don't tap for a while, or lose the same level twice, the Hint button glows to remind you.
  • Undo takes back your last move. If the last move was a bump, the heart comes back.
  • Blast lets you pick any arrow and sends it off the grid, even if it is blocked (the level then gives at most 2 stars).

You start with 2 Hints, 2 Undos and 1 Blast. When one runs out, its corner shows a green play icon: tap it to watch a short video for one more, or use coins.

How do I unlock arrow themes?

Open “Collection” on the home screen. Classic is ready from the start. Six more themes (Candy, Wood, Origami, Space, Neon and Gold) unlock one by one: each world Final you beat unlocks the next theme, and so do star milestones (60, 160, 280, 400, 520 and 640 stars) — whichever comes first. Tap an unlocked theme to use it.

What is “Color-blind patterns”?

Turn it on in Settings (the gear button). Arrows then also get clear patterns — dots, stripes or a center line — and a crisp white outline, so you can tell them apart without relying on color.

How do I connect to Game Center? Why don't I see my score?

Game Center is optional and the game works exactly the same without it. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (iPhone Settings › Game Center), the game connects quietly after you have finished five levels; the Game Center button then appears in the bottom-left corner of the home screen. Your Endless Mode best score goes to the leaderboard and you can earn achievements. To open the leaderboard, tap your best score on the Endless card on the home screen.

If your score doesn't show up: check that you are signed in to Game Center and connected to the internet; remember that only a new best score is sent; and give the leaderboard a few minutes to update. Endless Mode opens after you finish World 2. Scores and achievements earned while signed out wait on your device and are sent when you sign in. Game Center data is handled by Apple.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear button on the home screen and switch off Sound effects, Music or Vibration. Arrow Out never stops music you are playing from another app.
