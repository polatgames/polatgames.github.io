Candle Glow Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Candle Glow game (“the app”) uses and why. Candle Glow is developed by PolatGames (Türkiye), the data controller for the app. Questions: haticepolatgames@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Candle Glow has no accounts or sign-in of its own. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.
  • Your progress, stars, coins, lit venues and candle types are stored only on your device. We have no servers; this data never reaches us.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. To show ads, Google processes your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it) and technical information about your device.
  • You decide whether ads may be personalized, through a consent form and Apple's tracking permission. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
  • Game Center (a leaderboard and achievements) is optional and run by Apple. The game works exactly the same without it.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Coins and help items are earned by playing.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores only the following, and only on your device: the levels you finished and your stars in each, your coins, your Hint, Scissors and Extra Slot charges, your daily gift and play streak, the days you finished the Daily Challenge, your Lantern Street best score, the venues you have lit and your venue badges, the candle types you unlocked and the one you use, simple counters for achievements (for example how many levels you finished without using the tray), the intro and info cards you have seen, small counters that make a level a little easier after it was hard for you several times, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, look, color-blind mode and left-handed mode). Your answer to the consent form is also kept on your device so it is not asked again every time. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else (except as described in section 4 if you use Game Center). It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Candle Glow is free and supported by ads:

  • Video ads you choose to watch — for an extra Hint, Scissors or Extra Slot, to keep going once per level when the waiting tray is full, to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily gift.
  • An occasional full-screen ad — only when you tap “Next level” (at most once every three finished levels and at least two minutes apart; never in your first five levels, never after the tray filled up, never when you tap “Try again” or “Replay”) or when you leave a Lantern Street run for the home screen. Never while you are playing.
  • A banner ad at the very bottom of the game screen (levels, Daily Challenge and Lantern Street) and the venue map, in its own strip away from the counter, the tray and the buttons. There is no banner on the home screen, in Settings, in the intro, on result screens or during the “Venue lit!” celebration.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's permission window.
  • Device and app information — such as device model, operating system version, language, the app's vendor identifier (IDFV) and app version.
  • IP address — which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city).
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, whether they were tapped or watched, and diagnostic data about the ads such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, if you allow it, show ads that better match your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices: consent form and tracking permission

  • No window on first launch: the first time you open Candle Glow you just play the short tutorial. No permission window appears.
  • Consent form: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) right after the tutorial and before any ad loads. No ad loads until you answer. You can accept or refuse personalized ads.
  • Tracking permission: before the advertising identifier can be used, the app asks once, with Apple's App Tracking Transparency window, after you have finished level 3 (when the next level or the map opens). Nothing is offered in return for allowing it. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the identifier is not shared; you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Changing your mind: tap the gear button on the home screen to open Settings, then tap “Privacy choices”. Where the consent form applies (for example the EEA and the UK) it opens again and you can change your choice; elsewhere the Candle Glow page of iPhone Settings opens. You can also change tracking at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Whatever you choose, the game works exactly the same and you see the same number of ads.

4. Game Center (optional)

Candle Glow supports Apple's Game Center for a leaderboard (Lantern Street best score) and achievements. Game Center is an Apple service and completely optional: the game never asks you to sign in when it opens, and everything works the same without it. After you have finished five levels, the game quietly checks on the home screen whether you are already signed in to Game Center on your iPhone; it shows Apple's sign-in screen only if you tap your Lantern Street record on the home screen yourself.

If you are signed in, your Lantern Street best score and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center and shown with your Game Center name. Score and achievement data are managed by Apple, not by us, under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy); we never receive your Apple ID, email address or other personal information. If you are not signed in, your score and achievements stay on your device and are only sent if you sign in later. To sign out or change Game Center settings, use iPhone Settings › Game Center.

5. Legal basis (GDPR and KVKK)

  • Personalized ads and use of the advertising identifier: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5/1). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5/2-f) and, where the law requires, your consent.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. We share no data with anyone except for the ads described above (Google) and, if you choose to sign in, Game Center (Apple, see section 4). Google may process data on servers outside Türkiye and your country, including in the United States. These transfers are protected by safeguards such as Google's standard contractual clauses. For users in Türkiye, transfers abroad rely on the explicit consent you give through the consent form and the tracking permission.

7. How long data is kept

We do not store your data ourselves. Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Information processed by Google is kept under Google's retention policies, Game Center data under Apple's.

8. Children

Candle Glow is a game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, write to us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Art. 11) you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used for that purpose;
  • access your data and receive a copy;
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom data is transferred;
  • ask for correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
  • ask for deletion, destruction or restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest and to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing before the withdrawal);
  • data portability;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing;
  • complain to your local data protection authority in the EU or to Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Because there are no accounts or servers, we cannot link any data to you ourselves. For data processed by Google you can also use Google's tools: myadcenter.google.com. To exercise your rights, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com. We answer your request within 30 days at the latest.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
