Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Hint/Scissors/Extra Slot charges, streaks, your Lantern Street record, lit venues, badges, candle types and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from the beginning. Updating the app does not erase your progress. If you restore an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data, your progress comes back too.

Why do I see ads?

Candle Glow is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: watch one for an extra Hint, Scissors or Extra Slot, to keep going once per level when the tray is full, to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily gift. Now and then a short ad may appear when you tap “Next level” (never in your first five levels, never after the tray filled up, never when you tap “Try again” or “Replay”) or when you leave a Lantern Street run for the home screen — never while you are untangling wicks. A small banner sits in its own strip at the very bottom of the game screen and the map, well away from the counter, the tray and the buttons. A finger that slides down from a wick never taps the banner. The game also works without internet; there are just no ads and no video rewards.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (in regions where the consent form applies; elsewhere iPhone Settings opens). Or turn off tracking for Candle Glow in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Candle Glow uses your iPhone's language automatically. To choose another one, tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Language”, and pick one of 19 languages (choose “Phone language” to go back to your iPhone's language).

Why won't a wick come off when I tap it?

Something is lying on top of it. A wick can only be pulled when no other wick crosses over it — tap it and it gives a little tug while the wicks on top of it glow. Pull those first. A wick with a small lock on its pin also waits for its key (see below).

What is the waiting tray and what happens when it is full?

Above the counter, open molds wait for wicks of their color. A wick whose color has no open mold goes into the waiting tray under the counter (5 slots). When a mold of its color opens, it jumps in by itself. If the tray is full and one more wick needs a place, the level pauses: you can keep going (watch an ad once per level, or spend coins) — the tray gets 2 more slots — or try again. Tip: look at the upcoming mold colors next to the molds and pull wicks in a good order.

How do I get more stars?

Stars depend on how crowded the waiting tray got: the fewer wicks had to wait at the same time, the more stars. Finishing always gives at least one star. Using Scissors or an Extra Slot gives at most two, and continuing after a full tray gives one. Replay any finished level from the map to improve your stars.

How do I unlock the next venue?

Finish the venue's Final (its 20th level) and collect enough stars in total. If you need more, the map tells you how many — replay earlier levels and aim for three stars by using the tray less.

How do hidden wicks, locked pins and wax-coated wicks work?

Hidden wicks (from the Café) look grey while something lies on them and show their color once they are uncovered. A locked pin (from the Library) opens when you pull the wick with the matching key on its end. A wax-coated wick (from Home) needs two taps: the first cracks the wax (this works even if another wick lies on it), the second pulls it.

What do Hint, Scissors and Extra Slot do?

Hint makes the best wick to pull right now glow softly. Scissors cuts any wick free, even one lying under others: tap Scissors, then the wick. Extra Slot adds one slot to the tray for this level (up to two). You start with some free charges; when they run out you can get one with coins or by watching a short video (the green play sign).

What is Lantern Street?

An endless night street that opens after the Café is lit. Every counter you finish lights a lantern and the street goes on; the counters get busier as you go. The run ends when the tray is full. Your best run (lanterns lit) shows on the home screen.

How do I unlock candle types?

Each venue's Final gives you a new candle type: Beeswax, Lavender, Cinnamon, Ocean and Cherry. The Golden Candle unlocks when you have collected lots of stars. Choose your candle in Collection › Candles; it changes how every poured candle looks and the color of its flame.

Where can I see the venues I have lit?

Open Collection on the home screen and choose “My Venues”. Every fully lit venue stays there, glowing, and you can tap it to look at it. Your venue badges are under “Badges”.

What are color-blind mode and left-handed mode?

In Settings, color-blind mode adds a pattern (dots, stripes, stars and more) to every wick color and mold, so colors can be told apart without relying on color. Left-handed mode moves the help buttons and the remaining candle counter to the other side of the screen.

How do I connect to Game Center, and why might my score not show?

Game Center is optional. Sign in to Game Center in iPhone Settings › Game Center. After you have finished five levels, the game checks it quietly on the home screen; you can also tap your Lantern Street record on the home screen to open the leaderboard (and Apple's sign-in screen if you are not signed in). If your score or an achievement does not show yet, make sure you are signed in and online — results earned while signed out are sent the next time you open the home screen while signed in. Game Center can take a few minutes to update.
