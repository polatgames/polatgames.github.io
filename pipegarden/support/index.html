Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress. What happened?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, helpers, garden map, flower types and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, and Game Center does not store your progress either. Deleting the app, or moving to a new phone without restoring a backup, starts the game again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back.

Why do I see ads?

Pipe Garden is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. A small banner sits in its own strip at the bottom of the game screen and the garden map only. Video ads are always your choice: you can watch a short one to get one more helper or to double your coins or daily gift. Now and then a full-screen ad appears when you tap “Next level” — never in the first 5 levels, at most every 3 levels and never on “Retry”. There are no in-app purchases.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Open Settings in the game (the gear on the home screen) and tap “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens your phone's Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Pipe Garden. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the game, tap the button next to “Language” and choose one of 19 languages. Choose “Phone language” to follow your phone's language again.

What does “Perfect” mean, and how are moves counted?

Every tap on a pipe turns it a quarter turn, and every turn is one move. “Perfect” is the fewest turns needed to water every flower bed in that level — the top bar shows both, for example “Moves 7 · Perfect 6”. Finishing with the Perfect number of moves (or fewer) gives 3 stars. Turns made by Hint and Rain don't count as moves.

Do I have to use every pipe?

No. You only need to bring water to every flower bed. Some pipes are there just to make you think twice. If water runs out of an open pipe end it only makes a small puddle — there is no penalty and nothing to lose.

How do I get more stars?

Finish a level with the Perfect number of moves for 3 stars; a few moves more still gives 2 stars, and finishing always gives at least 1 star. Using Water Path caps the level at 2 stars. Replay any finished level from the map: your best result is kept, so you can only gain stars.

How do I open a new garden?

Each garden opens when you finish the previous garden's Final (level 20 of that garden, the trophy on the map) and have collected enough stars in total: 35 for the Rose Garden, 69 for the Stone Garden, 104 for the Desert Oasis and 138 for the Magic Garden. Tap a locked garden on the map to see how many stars you still need. Tip: replay earlier levels with fewer taps to earn Perfect stars.

How do bridges, stone channels and sprinklers work?

  • Stone channels are grey stone pieces that can't be turned. They guide the water; build your route around them.
  • Bridges let two streams cross: one goes over, the other under, and they never mix. Bridges don't turn.
  • Sprinklers start spinning when water reaches them and water every bed right next to them (above, below, left and right).
  • In the Magic Garden there can be two springs — use both to reach every bed.

What do Hint, Rain and Water Path do?

  • Hint turns the next wrong piece the right way, starting from where you are now (it doesn't count as a move).
  • Rain sends a little cloud that turns three wrong pieces the right way at once (no moves counted).
  • Water Path shows where the water should flow for 2 seconds (the level then gives at most 2 stars).

You start with 2 Hints, 2 Rains and 1 Water Path. When one runs out, its button shows a small green play icon: get one more for coins (Hint 30, Rain 60, Water Path 40) or by watching a short video. If you haven't tapped for a while, the Hint button glows gently as a reminder.

How do I change the turn direction?

Open Settings and choose “Clockwise” or “Counter-clockwise” under “Turn direction”. The Perfect number of each level adjusts to the direction you choose.

What is Endless Mode?

Endless Mode opens after you finish the Rose Garden's Final (or all 100 levels). Gardens come one after another and slowly grow bigger. Each one has a move limit (Perfect plus a few moves), and your score is how many gardens you revive in a row. Your best score is shown on the home screen; tap it to open the Game Center leaderboard.

How do I unlock flower types?

You start with the Daisy. The Tulip, Sunflower, Lavender, Cherry Blossom and Lotus unlock with the Finals of the five gardens, and the Golden Rose unlocks when you collect 240 stars. Choose your favorite in Collection (on the home screen): it changes the flowers that bloom, the color of the pipes and the shade of the grass.

What are color-blind mode and left-handed mode?

Both are in Settings. Color-blind mode adds clear patterns so you don't have to rely on color: watered tiles get stripes, dry soil gets dots, water gets a light line and watered beds get a small drop badge. Left-handed mode moves the move counter and flips the order of the helper buttons to the other side of the screen.

How do I connect to Game Center, and why can't I see my score?

Game Center is optional and the game works the same without it. Sign in from your phone's Settings › Game Center. After you have finished 5 levels, the game connects quietly the next time you are on the home screen, and a small Game Center icon appears in the bottom-left corner of the home screen. If your score doesn't show: make sure you are signed in to Game Center and online; only a new best Endless score is sent; it can take a few minutes to appear. Scores and achievements earned while you were not signed in are sent as soon as you sign in.
