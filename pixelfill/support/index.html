Frequently asked questions

I lost my progress. What happened?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, helpers, Gallery, themes and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so deleting the app, or moving to a new phone without restoring a backup, starts the game again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back.

Why do I see ads?

Pixel Fill is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. A small banner sits in its own strip on the game screen and the map only. Video ads are always your choice: you can watch a short one, for example, to get one more helper. There are no in-app purchases. Without an internet connection the game still works; there are just no ads and no video rewards.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Open Settings in the game and tap “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens your phone's Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Pixel Fill. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings in the game, tap “Language” and choose one of 19 languages. Choose “Device language” to go back to your phone's language.

What do the helpers do?

  • +1 Slot adds one more waiting slot for the current level.
  • Shuffle reshuffles the blocks in the tray. The new order is always solvable.
  • Undo takes back your last move.

You get 2 of each for free. After that, you can get more with coins or by watching a short video.

How do stars work? How do I open a new world?

You get 3 stars when you finish a level without helpers, 2 stars if you use one helper, and 1 star if you use more. Replaying a level keeps your best result. A new world opens when you finish the previous world's Final level and reach its star target. Tap a locked world on the map to see how many stars you still need.

What are the Gallery and block themes?

Every picture you complete is saved in your Gallery, so you can look back at everything you have filled. Block themes change how the blocks look; unlock them as you play and pick your favorite.

What is the Daily Picture?

Every day there is a new Daily Picture to fill, separate from the levels. Come back each day for a new one.

Is there a color-blind option?

Yes. Turn on the color-blind palette in Settings. Colors then become easier to tell apart, and each color also gets its own pattern, so you don't have to rely on color alone.

How do I use Game Center?

Game Center is optional and the game works the same without it. To use it, sign in from your phone's Settings › Game Center. Game Center data (player name, scores and achievements) is handled by Apple.

How can I contact you?

Write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.
