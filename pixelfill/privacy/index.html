Pixel Fill Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains, in plain words, what information the Pixel Fill game (“the app”) uses and why. Pixel Fill is made by PolatGames (Türkiye), which is the data controller for the app. If you have any questions, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There is no Pixel Fill account or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Your progress, stars, coins, gallery, themes and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers, and this data never reaches us.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. To show ads, Google processes your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking) and technical information about your device.
  • You decide whether ads may be personalized, using a consent form (where required) and Apple's tracking permission prompt. You can change your choice at any time.
  • Game Center is optional and run by Apple. The game works the same without it.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores only the following, and only on your device: which levels you have finished and how many stars you earned, your coins, your helpers (+1 Slot, Shuffle and Undo), the pictures in your Gallery, the Daily Pictures you completed, the block themes you unlocked and the one you use, which tutorial and tip cards you have already seen, counters that decide when an ad may appear, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language and color-blind palette). Your choice from the consent form is also saved on your device so that you are not asked again every time. This information does not leave your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Pixel Fill is free and supported by ads:

  • A banner ad in its own strip on the game screen and the map, kept apart from the puzzle and the buttons.
  • Video ads you choose to watch — for example to get one more helper. Watching them is always optional.
  • An occasional full-screen ad between levels — never in the first levels, at most every few wins, never after a lost round or on Retry.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's permission prompt.
  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, language and app version.
  • IP address — which may be used to estimate your coarse location (such as country or city).
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data — which ads were shown and whether they were tapped or watched, plus diagnostic data about ads such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse and, if you allow it, show ads that better match your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can read how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices: consent form and tracking permission

  • Nothing is asked at first launch: you first play the short tutorial. No ads are loaded during it.
  • Consent form: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (or another region where it is required), the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) after the first-launch tutorial, before any ad is loaded. No ads are loaded until you answer. You can accept or refuse personalized ads.
  • Tracking permission: the app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission only once, after you finish level 3. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier is not shared. Nothing about the game changes: you still see ads, they are just less personalized.
  • Changing your mind: open Settings in the game and tap “Privacy choices”. Where the consent form applies, the form opens again and you can change your choice; in other regions, the Pixel Fill page of your phone's Settings opens. You can also change the tracking permission any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

4. Game Center (optional)

Pixel Fill supports Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. Using it is entirely optional and the game works the same without it. If you are signed in, your Game Center player name, scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy), not by us. We do not receive your Apple ID, email address or other personal information. You can sign out or change Game Center settings in iPhone Settings › Game Center.

5. In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Coins, helpers and themes are earned by playing (or by watching an optional video).

6. Legal basis (GDPR and KVKK)

  • Personalized ads and use of the advertising identifier: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5/1). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5/2-f), and your consent where the law requires it.

7. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ads described above (Google) and Game Center if you use it (Apple), we share no data with anyone. Google may process data on servers outside Türkiye and outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. For users in Türkiye, the transfer abroad is based on the consent you give through the consent form and tracking permission.

8. How long data is kept

We do not store your data ourselves. Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies; Game Center data according to Apple's.

9. Children

Pixel Fill is not directed at children and is intended for ages 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

10. Your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Art. 11), you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and get information and a copy of it (access);
  • ask for incorrect data to be corrected;
  • ask for your data to be deleted, or for its processing to be restricted;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing before the withdrawal);
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom data is transferred;
  • complain to your local data protection authority or to Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Because there is no account or server, we cannot link any data to you ourselves. For data processed by Google you can also use myadcenter.google.com. To use your rights, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
