Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Hint/Magnifier/Clear charges, Hidden Words, streaks, records, grid themes and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app, or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back too.

Why do I see ads?

Word Scout is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: watch one for an extra Hint, Magnifier or Clear, to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily gift. A short ad may also appear from time to time after you tap “Next level” (never in your first five levels and never when you tap “Retry”) or when you leave the end-of-round screen of Speed Mode or Endless Mode — never while you are searching. A small banner sits in its own strip at the bottom of the game screen and the map, well away from the grid and the buttons; sliding your finger off the grid never taps it. Without an internet connection the game still works; there are just no ads, and video gifts are not available.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens iPhone Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Word Scout. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language? Do the puzzles change too?

Word Scout follows your iPhone's language automatically. To pick another one, tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Language”, and choose from 19 languages (or “Phone language” to follow your iPhone again). The puzzles always come in the game's language: every language has its own word lists and its own alphabet, so a Turkish grid has Turkish words and letters, a Japanese grid has kana, and so on. Your levels, stars and coins stay exactly as they were when you switch.

How do I select a word?

Put your finger on the first letter and slide it in a straight line to the last letter, then lift. You don't have to be precise: the selection snaps to the nearest straight line (across, down or diagonal), and if your finger slides off the grid it simply stops at the last letter. You can also tap the first letter and then tap the last letter. Either direction counts. If the word is on the list, it gets a colored capsule and is crossed off the list.

Can words go diagonally or backwards?

In the first world words only run left to right and top to bottom (right to left in Arabic). From World 2 (Diagonal Dash) words can also run diagonally, and from World 3 (Flip Side) they can be written backwards too — right to left, bottom to top and reversed diagonals. A short card tells you when something new arrives.

What is the Hidden Word?

From level 3 on, every level hides one extra word that fits the theme but is not on the list — it shows as a “?” box at the end of the list. Find it for a golden capsule and extra coins. It is never required to finish a level. Every 10th level hides two of them. If you finish without it, the result screen tells you it was there (but not which word).

How does the list work in Mystery Woods?

In World 4 (Mystery Woods) the list does not show the words. You see the theme and, for each word, a row of empty boxes as long as the word, with only its first letter given. When you find a word, its boxes fill in.

How do stars and the target time work?

Every level has a generous target time, shown next to the stopwatch at the top (for example “1:12 · Target 1:30”). There is no countdown and you can never lose. Finish within the target for three stars, a little over it for two stars, and finishing always gives at least one star. Using the Magnifier gives at most two stars for that try. Replay any level from the map: if you finish faster, your stars go up.

How do I unlock the next world?

Beat the Final (level 20) of the world before it and collect enough stars in total. If a world is still locked, the map shows how many more stars you need. The easiest way to earn them is to replay earlier levels and beat their target time.

What do Hint, Magnifier and Clear do?

  • Hint lights up the first letter of a word you haven't found yet, for a few seconds.
  • Magnifier shows a whole word for a moment. Using it gives at most two stars for that try.
  • Clear fades out part of the letters that don't belong to any word you still need, so the grid gets tidier.

You start with two Hints, two Magnifiers and one Clear. When a helper runs out, its corner shows a green play sign: get one more with coins or as a free gift by watching a short video. If you haven't found a word for a while, the Hint button glows gently as a reminder.

What are Speed Mode and Endless Mode?

Speed Mode: find as many words as you can in 90 seconds on small grids; when a grid is done, the next one appears. Your record is shown on the home screen. Endless Mode opens after you beat the World 2 Final: you start with 60 seconds, every word you find adds a few seconds (longer words add more), and the grids slowly grow. The round ends when the time runs out; your best score is shown on the home screen.

How do I unlock grid themes?

Classic is yours from the start. Chalkboard, Neon, Forest, Ocean and Space unlock when you beat the Final of Worlds 1 to 5, and Gold unlocks when you collect 240 stars. Pick the one you like in Collection on the home screen; locked themes show how to unlock them.

What are Color-blind mode, Left-handed mode and Big letters?

  • Color-blind mode gives every found word a clear pattern and a thick outline, so words can be told apart without colors.
  • Left-handed mode moves the helper buttons and the stopwatch to the other side.
  • Big letters makes the letters larger and spaces the grid a little more.

You find all three in Settings (gear button on the home screen).

How do I connect to Game Center, and why isn't my score showing?

Game Center is optional. Sign in once in iPhone Settings › Game Center. After you have finished five levels, Word Scout connects by itself on the home screen, and the Game Center icon appears in the bottom-left corner of the home screen. Tap your Speed Mode record or Endless Mode best score on the home screen to open its leaderboard (if you aren't signed in, Apple's sign-in screen appears there). If a score or achievement isn't showing yet: make sure you are signed in to Game Center and connected to the internet, then open the home screen again — scores and achievements earned while you were offline or signed out are kept on your device and sent automatically. Game Center can also take a few minutes to update.
