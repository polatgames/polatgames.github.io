Twirl Tap Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Twirl Tap game (“the app”) uses and why. Twirl Tap is developed by PolatGames (Türkiye), the data controller for the app. If you have any questions, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There is no Twirl Tap account or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.
  • Game Center (leaderboards and achievements) is optional and run by Apple. The game works exactly the same without it.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google processes your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it) and technical information about your device.
  • You decide whether ads can be personalized, through a consent form and Apple's tracking permission. You can change your choice at any time.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Coins and themes are earned by playing.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: your level progress, stars and best times, coins and boosters, daily reward and daily streak, Daily Challenge, Speed Mode and Endless Mode results, the badges and themes in your Collection, whether you have finished the first-time tutorial, counters used to space out ads, Speed Mode and Endless Mode scores and achievements that are waiting to be sent to Game Center, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, look, reduce spin and color-blind mode). Your choice from the consent form is also saved on your device so that you are not asked again every time. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Twirl Tap is free and is supported by ads:

  • Video ads you choose to watch — always optional: you tap a video button yourself, for example for a booster, extra seconds when time runs out, or double coins.
  • A full-screen ad between levels from time to time — only between rounds, never while you are playing a level, and not during your first wins.
  • A banner ad at the bottom of the game screen and the world map. There is no banner on the home screen, in Settings, in the Collection or on result screens.

No ads are shown during the first-launch tutorial. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's permission window.
  • Device and app information — such as device model, operating system version, language, the app's identifier for vendors (IDFV), and app version.
  • IP address — which can be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city).
  • Ad interaction data — such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped or watched, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance of the ads.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, if you agree, show ads that are more relevant to you. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices: consent form and tracking permission

  • Nothing is asked at first launch: you first play the short tutorial. No ads are shown during it.
  • Consent form: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the app shows a consent form from Google (User Messaging Platform) after the tutorial, right before the first ad would load. No ads are loaded until your choice allows it. You can agree to or refuse personalized ads.
  • Tracking permission: Before the advertising identifier can be used, the app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency window. This question is not shown at first launch; it appears once, after you finish level 3. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Changing your mind: In the game, tap the gear button on the home screen to open Settings, then tap “Privacy choices”. Where the consent form applies (for example in the EEA and UK), this opens it again so you can change your choice; elsewhere it opens the iPhone settings for Twirl Tap. You can also change tracking permission at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

The game works the same whatever you choose.

4. Game Center (optional)

Twirl Tap supports Apple's Game Center for Speed Mode and Endless Mode leaderboards and for achievements. Game Center is a service of Apple, and using it is entirely optional: the game never asks you to sign in when you first open it, and everything works exactly the same without it. If you are already signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game connects quietly after you have finished five levels.

If you are signed in, your Speed Mode and Endless Mode best scores and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center and shown with your Game Center name. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy), not by us; we do not receive your Apple ID, email address or any other personal information. Scores and achievements you earn while signed out are kept on your device and sent after you sign in. You can sign out or change Game Center settings in iPhone Settings › Game Center.

5. Legal basis (GDPR and KVKK)

  • Personalized ads and use of the advertising identifier: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1) – explicit consent). You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)), and your consent where the law requires it.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ads described above (Google) and Game Center if you are signed in (Apple, see section 4), we do not share any data with anyone. Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

7. Data retention

We do not store any of your data ourselves. Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies; Game Center data is kept according to Apple's.

8. Children

Twirl Tap is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it;
  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • ask for incorrect data to be corrected;
  • ask for your data to be deleted or restricted;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing done before withdrawal);
  • data portability;
  • learn the third parties to whom data is transferred, inside or outside the country;
  • claim compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing;
  • file a complaint with a data protection authority — for example your local authority in the EU, or the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye.

Because we do not have any account or server, we cannot link data to you ourselves. For data processed by Google you can also use Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. To exercise your rights, email haticepolatgames@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
