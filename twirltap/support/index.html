Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, streaks, your Collection and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app, or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back too.

Why do I see ads?

Twirl Tap is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: you can watch one to get a booster, extra seconds when time runs out, or double coins. A short ad may also appear between levels from time to time, but only after you tap “Next level” — never while you are playing, never right after you lose a level, and not during your first five wins. There are no banner ads.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens iPhone Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Twirl Tap. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear button on the home screen, tap “Language” and choose one of 19 languages. Choose “Automatic” at the top of the list to go back to your phone's language.

The circle spins too fast for me. What can I do?

Open Settings (the gear button) and turn on “Reduce spin”. The circle turns more slowly in every level and mode, and the twinkling background stays still. Your stars and records still count as usual.

I find the colors hard to tell apart.

Open Settings and turn on “Color-blind mode”. In the Colors world, where you tap tiles from light to dark, the shades switch from green to blue, which is easier to tell apart for most types of color blindness.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear button on the home screen and switch off Sound, Music or Vibration. Twirl Tap never stops music you are playing from another app.
