Frequently asked questions

What happens if my progress is lost?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Flag Album and settings) is saved only on your device. There is no account or cloud backup, so if you delete the app or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, your progress starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. An iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data will bring it back when restored.

Why do I see ads?

FlagQuiz is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. You can watch optional video ads to earn rewards — an extra life, a 50/50 hint or double coins. A short ad may appear between levels from time to time, but never while you are answering a question. The first five levels have no ads between levels.

Can I stop personalized ads?

Yes. Open Settings in the game and tap “Privacy preferences” to change your consent choice (where available), or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn tracking off for FlagQuiz. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Scroll to the bottom of the home screen, tap the language button with the globe icon and pick a language. Choose “Device language” at the top of the list to follow your iPhone's language again.

How do I turn sound or vibration off?

Tap the gear button at the top right of the home screen and switch Sound or Vibration off.
