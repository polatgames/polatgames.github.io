Frequently asked questions

What happens if my progress is lost?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Flag Album and settings) is saved only on your device. There is no account or cloud backup, so if you delete the app or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, your progress starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. An iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data will bring it back when restored. Game Center only keeps your Time Attack best score and achievements, not your level progress.

Why do I see ads?

FlagQuiz is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. You can choose to watch video ads to earn rewards — an extra life, a 50/50 hint or double coins; they never play unless you tap to watch. A full-screen ad may appear from time to time only after you finish a level (never during the first five levels), never in the middle of one. A small banner ad is shown at the bottom of the game screen and the world map.

Are there any in-app purchases?

No. FlagQuiz has no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is free and is earned by playing.

Can I stop personalized ads?

Yes. Open Settings in the game and tap “Privacy preferences” to change your consent choice (where available), or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn tracking off for FlagQuiz. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

By default the game uses your iPhone's language. To pick another one, scroll to the bottom of the home screen and tap the language button with the globe icon, or open Settings and tap “Language”. Choose “Device language” at the top of the list to follow your iPhone's language again.

How do I turn sound or vibration off?

Tap the gear button at the top right of the home screen and switch Sound, Music or Vibration off.

How do I connect to Game Center?

After you have finished 5 levels, the game connects to Game Center by itself if your iPhone is already signed in to Game Center — no window pops up. To sign in, go to iPhone Settings › Game Center. To see the leaderboard, tap your Time Attack record on the home screen. Game Center is optional; if you don't use it, you lose nothing.

Why doesn't my score appear on the leaderboard?

  • Not signed in: check that you are signed in under iPhone Settings › Game Center. Your best score is kept on your device and sent when you sign in.
  • No internet: scores are sent when your iPhone is online again.
  • It can take a while: Game Center leaderboards sometimes update with a delay.
  • Screen Time: if Game Center is restricted in Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions, scores can't be sent.
