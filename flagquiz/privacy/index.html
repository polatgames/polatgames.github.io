FlagQuiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the FlagQuiz game (“the app”) uses and why. FlagQuiz is developed by PolatGames (Türkiye), the data controller for the app. If you have any questions, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There are no accounts of our own. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information.
  • There are no in-app purchases. The app does not sell anything and does not process any payments.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, Google processes your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it) and technical information about your device.
  • You decide whether ads can be personalized, through a consent form and Apple's tracking permission. You can change your choice at any time.
  • Game Center is optional. If you use it, your Time Attack best score and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, using the standard iOS app storage (UserDefaults):

  • Game progress: levels and stars, coins, lives, the flags unlocked in your Flag Album, daily streaks, best scores, the days you played the Daily Challenge and collected the daily reward, and how many “+1 life” ads you watched today.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, language, appearance (light or dark) and the color-blind option.

There is no account and we do not run any servers. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else (except the Game Center data described in section 4, if you use Game Center). It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FlagQuiz is free and is supported by ads: rewarded videos that play only when you choose to watch one (for example to get an extra life or double coins), a full-screen ad from time to time only after you finish a level, and a banner ad at the bottom of the game screen and the world map. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's permission window.
  • Device and app information — such as device model, operating system version, language, the app's identifier for vendors (IDFV), and app version.
  • IP address — which can be used to estimate your general location (such as your country or city).
  • Ad interaction data — such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped or watched, and diagnostic data such as crashes and performance of the ads.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, if you agree, show ads that are more relevant to you. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices: consent form and tracking permission

  • Consent form: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the app shows a consent form from Google (User Messaging Platform) after you finish the first level, before any ad is loaded. You can agree to or refuse personalized ads.
  • Tracking permission: The app asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency window only after you complete level 3. The advertising identifier is not used unless you allow it. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Changing your mind: Open the app's Settings and tap “Privacy preferences”. This opens the consent form again; in regions where the form is not required, it opens the app's page in iPhone Settings, where you can change the tracking permission. You can also change it in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

The game works the same whatever you choose.

4. Game Center (optional)

FlagQuiz can use Apple's Game Center for a Time Attack leaderboard and achievements. The game asks for Game Center only after you have finished 5 levels. If your iPhone is already signed in to Game Center, the game connects quietly; the Game Center sign-in screen only appears if you tap the leaderboard. When you are signed in, your Time Attack best score and your achievements are sent to Game Center, together with your Game Center player profile managed by Apple. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. We do not receive your Game Center data on any server of our own. Game Center is not required: if you do not use it, you lose nothing and the whole game works the same.

5. Legal basis (GDPR and KVKK)

  • Personalized ads and use of the advertising identifier: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1) – explicit consent). You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)), and your consent where the law requires it.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share any data with anyone other than Google for the advertising described above (and Apple's Game Center, only if you use it). Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google uses safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

7. Data retention

We do not store any of your data ourselves. Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies, and Game Center data according to Apple's policies.

8. Children

FlagQuiz is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and request information about it;
  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • ask for incorrect data to be corrected;
  • ask for your data to be deleted or restricted;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing done before withdrawal);
  • data portability;
  • learn the third parties to whom data is transferred, inside or outside the country;
  • claim compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing;
  • file a complaint with a data protection authority — for example your local authority in the EU, or the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) in Türkiye.

Because we do not have any account or server, we cannot link data to you ourselves. For data processed by Google you can also use Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. To exercise your rights, email haticepolatgames@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
