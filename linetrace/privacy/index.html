Line Trace Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Line Trace game (“the app”) uses and why. Line Trace is made by PolatGames (Türkiye), which is the data controller for the app. If you have any questions, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There is no Line Trace account or sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Game Center (leaderboard and achievements) is optional and run by Apple. The game works the same without it.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers, and this data never reaches us.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. To show ads, Google processes your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow it) and technical information about your device.
  • You decide whether ads may be personalized, using a consent form and Apple's tracking permission prompt. You can change your choice at any time.
  • There are no in-app purchases. Coins and line themes are earned by playing.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores only the following, and only on your device: which levels you have finished and how many stars you earned, your coins, your Hint, Undo and Skip charges, your daily reward and daily streak, the days you completed the Daily Challenge, your Endless Mode scores, the line themes you unlocked and the one you use, which tutorial and introduction cards you have already seen, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, appearance, colorblind-friendly palette and thick lines). Your consent choice from the consent form is also saved on your device so that you are not asked again every time. This information does not leave your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

Line Trace is free and supported by ads:

  • Video ads you choose to watch — for an extra Hint, Undo or Skip, to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily reward.
  • A full-screen ad between levels from time to time — only after you tap “Next level”, never while you are drawing, and never during your first five levels.
  • A banner ad in its own strip at the bottom of the level screen and the map.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in Apple's permission prompt.
  • Device and app information — such as device model, operating system version, language, the app's vendor identifier (IDFV) and app version.
  • IP address — which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city).
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, and whether they were tapped or watched, plus diagnostic data about ads such as crashes and performance.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure how ads perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you allow it, show you ads that better match your interests. Google acts under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices: consent form and tracking permission

  • Consent form: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) after the first-launch tutorial, before any ad is loaded. No ads are loaded until you answer. You can accept or refuse personalized ads.
  • Tracking permission: before the advertising identifier is used, the app asks for permission with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, once, after you finish level 3. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the identifier is not shared. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized.
  • Changing your mind: in the game, tap the gear button on the home screen to open Settings, then tap “Privacy choices”. Where the consent form applies (for example in the EEA and the UK), the form opens again and you can change your choice; in other regions, the Line Trace page of iPhone Settings opens. You can change the tracking permission any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Whatever you choose, the game works exactly the same, and you see the same number of ads.

4. Game Center (optional)

Line Trace supports Apple's Game Center for an Endless Mode leaderboard and achievements. Game Center is a service of Apple, and using it is entirely optional: the game never asks you to sign in when it starts, and everything works the same without it. After you have finished a few levels, the game checks quietly whether you are already signed in to Game Center on your iPhone; it only shows Apple's sign-in screen if you tap the leaderboard yourself.

If you are signed in, your Endless Mode best score and the achievements you earn are sent to Apple's Game Center and shown with your Game Center name. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy), not by us; we do not receive your Apple ID, email address or any other personal information. If you are not signed in, your scores and achievements simply stay on your device and are sent later only if you sign in. You can sign out or change Game Center settings in iPhone Settings › Game Center.

5. Legal basis (GDPR and KVKK)

  • Personalized ads and use of the advertising identifier: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5/1). You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Non-personalized ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5/2-f), and your consent where the law requires it.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the ads described above (Google) and Game Center if you choose to sign in (Apple, see section 4), we share no data with anyone. Google may process data on servers outside Türkiye and outside your country, including in the United States. These transfers are covered by safeguards such as Google's standard contractual clauses. For users in Türkiye, the transfer abroad is based on the consent you give through the consent form and tracking permission.

7. How long data is kept

We do not store your data ourselves. Information on your device stays until you delete the app. Information processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

8. Children

Line Trace is a game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

9. Your rights

Under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK, Art. 11), you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it;
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether data is used for that purpose;
  • access your data and get a copy of it;
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom data is transferred;
  • ask for incomplete or inaccurate data to be corrected;
  • ask for data to be erased or destroyed, or for its processing to be restricted;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing before the withdrawal);
  • data portability;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing;
  • complain to your local data protection authority in the EU or to Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

Because there is no account or server, we cannot link any data to you ourselves. For data processed by Google you can also use Google's tools: myadcenter.google.com. To use your rights, write to haticepolatgames@gmail.com. We will answer your request within 30 days at the latest.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
