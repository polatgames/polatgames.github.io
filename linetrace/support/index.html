Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Hint/Undo/Skip charges, streaks, your Collection and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app, or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. Restoring an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data brings your progress back too.

Why do I see ads?

Line Trace is free and is supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: you can watch one for an extra Hint, Undo or Skip, to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily reward. A short ad may also appear between levels from time to time, but only after you tap “Next level” — never while you are drawing, and not during your first five levels. A small banner sits in its own strip at the bottom of the level screen and the map, away from the shape and the buttons. Without an internet connection the game still works; there are just no ads, and video rewards are not available.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (where the consent form applies; elsewhere it opens iPhone Settings). Or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for Line Trace. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear button on the home screen, tap “Language” and choose one of 19 languages. Choose “Phone language” to go back to your phone's language.

I'm stuck. Where should I start?

The goal is to draw every line in one stroke without going over a line twice. If there is no free line left to go on, your drawing gently rewinds and you can try again. A few tips:

  • Find the right start: count how many lines meet at each dot (a double line counts as two). If exactly two dots have an odd number of lines, you must start at one of them — and you will finish at the other. If every dot has an even number, you can start anywhere and you will end where you began.
  • Lifting your finger is fine: put it back on the end of your line to keep going. Tapping another dot starts a new try.
  • Undo takes back your last three lines, so you can try a different turn.
  • Hint: the first tap lights up a dot you can start from; more taps show the next lines of the solution. If you get stuck a lot, the Hint button glows to remind you.

How do I get more stars?

You get 3 stars when you finish a level without a hint and without starting over. One or two restarts, or one hint, gives 2 stars; more than that gives 1 star. Starting again from another dot counts as a restart. You can replay any finished level from the map — your best result is kept, and new stars also give you their coins.

I skipped a level. Can I go back to it?

Yes. A skipped level counts as 1 star and gives no coins. Open the map, tap that level and play it again to earn more stars and coins.

How do I unlock a new world?

Each world opens when you finish the final level of the previous world and have collected enough stars in total. The map shows how many more stars you need. If you are short, replay earlier levels to raise their stars.

How do double lines and one-way lines work?

Thick double lines must be drawn twice: each pass fills one of its two lanes. You can turn around and go straight back over it, or come back to it later in the same stroke. Lines with arrows can only be drawn in the direction the arrow shows.

How do I unlock line themes?

Open “Collection” from the home screen and choose “Lines”. Neon is ready from the start. Finishing the final level of each world unlocks a new theme, and two more unlock at 100 and 300 total stars. Tap an unlocked theme to use it.

What are “Colorblind-friendly” and “Thick lines”?

Both are in Settings (the gear button). Colorblind-friendly draws your lines in one easy-to-see blue in every world, makes drawn lines clearly thicker than the lines you still have to draw, and a blocked move flashes orange instead of red. Thick lines makes the lines and dots of every shape thicker and easier to see.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Tap the gear button on the home screen and switch off Sound, Music or Vibration. Line Trace never stops music you are playing from another app.
