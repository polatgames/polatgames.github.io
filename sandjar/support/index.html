Frequently asked questions

What happens if I lose my progress?

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Guide/Undo/Smooth charges, streaks, records, themes, your Jar Shelf, Workshop jars and settings) is stored only on your device. There is no account or cloud save, so if you delete the app or move to a new device without restoring a full device backup, the game starts again from level 1. Updating the app does not erase your progress. If you restore an iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) that includes app data, your progress comes back too.

Why do I see ads?

Sand Jar is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. Video ads are always your choice: watch one for an extra Guide, Undo or Smooth, to double the coins at the end of a level, or to double your daily reward. Now and then a short ad may appear when you tap “Next level” (never in your first five levels, never when you tap “Again”, never in the Workshop) or when you leave the end-of-round screen of Endless Mode — never while you are pouring sand. A small banner sits in its own strip at the very bottom of the game screen and the map, well away from the jar, the sand bags and the buttons. If your finger slides down toward the banner while pouring, the sand simply stops; the banner is never tapped by accident. The game also works without internet; there are just no ads and no video rewards.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Privacy choices” to change your consent choice (in regions where the consent form applies; elsewhere iPhone Settings opens). Or turn off tracking for Sand Jar in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change the language?

Sand Jar uses your iPhone's language automatically. To choose another one, tap the gear button on the home screen, then “Language”, and pick one of 19 languages (choose “Phone language” to go back to your iPhone's language).

How do I pour sand and build a hill?

Tap a sand bag to pick a color — the bag floats up. Then press and hold anywhere over the jar: sand flows down from your finger for as long as you hold, and stops when you lift it. Slide your finger left and right to move the stream and spread the sand into an even layer. To build a hill, hold on one spot: the sand piles up into a cone, and when the slope gets too steep it slides down to the sides by itself — just like real sand. Pour a little to the side of a hill to make a valley. The faint dashed lines in the jar show where each layer of the goal picture should end; they fade as your sand reaches them.

How is “Match %” calculated, and how do I get more stars?

Match compares the sand in your jar with the goal picture (tap the small goal card at the top right to see it bigger). Every color of the picture counts equally, so a small sun matters as much as a big sky, and sand that is only one or two grains away from the right spot still counts partly. While you pour, Match shows how well the sand poured so far fits. Finishing a jar always gives at least one star; about 82% Match gives two stars, about 93% gives three, and 95% or more is a golden Perfect Match. To improve your stars, replay any level from the map: a better match raises your stars and the jar on your shelf.

What happens if a sand bag runs out?

Each bag shows how much sand is left on the small bar above it. If you try to pour from an empty bag it gives a little shake and the other bags glow. Don't worry: the bags together always hold more than enough sand to fill the jar, so you can never get stuck. Use Undo to take back a pour that used too much of one color.

How do I unlock a new world?

Finish the previous world's Final (level 20) and collect enough stars in total. If a world is still locked, its card on the map shows how many more stars you need. The easiest way to collect stars is to replay earlier levels with a better match.

What do Guide, Undo and Smooth do?

  • Guide shows your next pour: which bag to use, where to pour (a faint stream) and how high the sand should go (a glowing line).
  • Undo takes back your last pour (or Stick poke) — the sand floats back up into its bag.
  • Smooth gently levels the little bumps on top of your sand.

You start with 2 Guides, 2 Undos and 1 Smooth. When a helper runs out, buy one with coins or watch a short video for a free one (look for the green “play” badge). If you seem stuck or haven't poured for a while, the Guide button glows gently as a reminder.

How do I use the Stick?

The Stick arrives in World 5 (Master) and is free to use. Tap the Stick button next to the helpers, then put your finger on the jar and drag it down along the glass: the colors on top are pushed down into the layers below, making drops and petals. Undo takes a poke back too.

What is the Workshop, and how do I share a jar?

The Workshop opens after level 10. Pick any jar shape you have met and any sand theme you have unlocked, then pour freely with unlimited sand — no goal, no clock. Tap “Done” to put a cork on it, then “Put it on my shelf” or “Share”. Share opens the iOS share sheet with a picture of your jar; the picture is made on your phone and we never upload it anywhere.

How do the Jar Shelf and deleting work?

Every level you finish puts its jar — exactly as you made it, with your best match — on the Jar Shelf, sorted by world; empty spots show faint outlines. Tap a jar to see it bigger with its match. Workshop jars stand on their own shelf at the top; tap one and choose “Remove from shelf” to delete it. Level jars can't be deleted, but replaying a level with a better match replaces its jar.

How do I unlock sand themes?

Classic Desert is yours from the start. Pastel, Ocean, Forest, Neon and Night Sky unlock when you finish the Finals of worlds 1 to 5, and Gold Glitter unlocks when you have collected enough stars in total (the Collection shows how many). Open the Collection (the trophy button on the home screen's progress card) and tap a theme to use it — it changes the tone, grain and glitter of the sand, not the goal picture.

What is Endless Mode?

Endless Mode opens when you finish World 2 (or all 100 levels). Sand pictures come one after another and slowly become more detailed. A jar with two or more stars takes you to the next one; below that, the round ends and you see all the jars of your round on a little shelf. Your score is the number of jars you made in a row, and your best score is shown on the home screen.

What are color-blind mode and left-handed mode?

Both are in Settings. Color-blind mode gives every sand color its own fine pattern (stripes, dots and more), in the jar, on the bags and on the goal card, so you can tell the colors apart without relying on color. Left-handed mode flips the order of the sand bags and the helper buttons.

How do I connect to Game Center, and why might my score not show?

Game Center is optional; the game works the same without it. Sign in once in iPhone Settings › Game Center. After you have finished five levels, Sand Jar connects quietly on the home screen and a small Game Center button appears in the bottom-left corner of the home screen. Tap your Endless Mode best score on the home screen to open the leaderboard. If your score doesn't show yet:

  • you may not be signed in to Game Center, or Game Center may be restricted in Screen Time;
  • you may be offline — scores and achievements are stored on your device and sent the next time you open the home screen with an internet connection;
  • only your best score counts, and leaderboards can take a few minutes to update.
